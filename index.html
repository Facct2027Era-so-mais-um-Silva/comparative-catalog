About this page

Images generated by three text-to-image models (Meta, Google, Qwen) from Portuguese prompts built around Brazilian surnames, with or without gender and region markers. Each card is one prompt: click it to see the output of every model side by side, and click an image to enlarge it. The English line under each prompt is a translation for readers; the prompts were run in Portuguese.

Use the filters to combine surnames, genders, regions and models. Several values can be selected at once.

About these graphs

Each panel shows the images produced by one model, embedded with OpenCLIP and projected to two dimensions with UMAP (one image per prompt, 198 points per model). Points that sit close together are images that CLIP represents similarly. Every panel has its own coordinates (the axis ranges differ), so positions should not be compared across panels. Color and shape mark the region named in the prompt; grey circles are prompts that name no region.

Hover a point to preview its image and prompt, click it to open the side-by-side comparison of all models for that prompt, drag to zoom and double-click to reset.
