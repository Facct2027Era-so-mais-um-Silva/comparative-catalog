About this page

Images generated by three popular generative models (Meta AI, Google Gemini, Qwen) from Portuguese prompts built around Brazilian surnames, with or without gender and region markers. Each card is one prompt: click it to see the output of every model side by side, and click an image to enlarge it. The English line under each prompt is a translation for readers; the prompts were run in Portuguese.

Use the filters to combine surnames, genders, regions and models. Several values can be selected at once.
